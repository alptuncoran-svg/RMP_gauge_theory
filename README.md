# RMP_gauge_theory
aproaching riemannian motion policies through the lens of gauge theory


This repo is me experimenting with some concepts regarding RMP's, specially borrowing the concept of "vielbeins" from gauge theory (sub field of general relativity). What I do can be viewed as warping our flat task space in a way that it is more like a mountain range, where the simplest path is usually just traveling around the mountains rather than over them. I haven't invented anything knew but I believe pedagogically speaking it is a much clearer way to teach the concept for people who aren't mathematicians or differential geometers. Also I believe this to potentially better in terms of computational efficiency due to the fact that we no longer have to compute Christoffel symbols of the first kind to define our Coriolis matrix which is a big plus.
